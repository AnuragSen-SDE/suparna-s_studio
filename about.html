Hello, I'm Suparna

What started as a love for colors and creativity grew into a passion for makeup artistry. I believe every face tells a unique story, and my job is to enhance your natural beauty while honoring who you are.

Over the years, I've had the privilege of working with brides on their most important day, creating glamorous looks for celebrations, and pushing creative boundaries with editorial work. Each client brings a new canvas, and I approach every session with patience, precision, and a genuine desire to make you feel confident.

Alongside makeup, my creative spirit extends to handcrafted goods — from delicate rakhi and artisan earrings to hand-printed kurtas. These pieces carry the same care and attention to detail that I bring to every makeup brushstroke.
